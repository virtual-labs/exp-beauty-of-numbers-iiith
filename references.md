1. **David M. Burton, Elementary Number Theory (7th Edition).**
   - Comprehensive introduction to number theory, including perfect numbers and divisors.

2. **Richard K. Guy, The Book of Numbers.**
   - Accessible exploration of beautiful properties and patterns in numbers.

3. **Wikipedia: Continued Fraction**
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continued_fraction
   - Theory, examples, and applications of continued fractions.

4. **Paul Zeitz, The Art and Craft of Problem Solving.**
   - Problem-solving strategies and number theory puzzles.

5. **NPTEL: Introduction to Number Theory**
   - https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111/106/111106139/
   - Video lectures and notes on number theory topics.
